# E-Commerce_Sales_Customer_Analysis
Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce transactions to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, sales channels, geographic performance, order outcomes and operational KPIs.
It demonstrates how raw transaction data can be converted into management-level insights using Excel.

Business Problem
An e-commerce business wants to answer:
Which categories generate the most sales and profit?
Which sales channel performs best?
Which cities contribute the most revenue?
How many customers are repeat customers?
What is the average order value?
What percentage of orders are returned or cancelled?
How long does delivery take?
Which areas should management prioritize?
Objectives
Measure overall sales and profitability.
Analyze category and channel performance.
Identify high-performing cities.
Measure customer repeat-purchase behavior.
Analyze returns and cancellations.
Track monthly sales trends.
Create business recommendations from the analysis.

📌 Key KPIs
KPI	Definition
Total Sales	Sum of transaction sales
Total Profit	Sales − Cost
Profit Margin	Profit ÷ Sales
Average Order Value	Sales ÷ Orders
Repeat Customer Rate	Customers with ≥2 orders ÷ total customers
Return Rate	Returned orders ÷ total orders
Cancellation Rate	Cancelled orders ÷ total orders
Average Delivery Days	Average delivery duration for delivered orders
 Key Findings
Using the included synthetic dataset:
Electronics is the leading category by sales.
Mobile App is the strongest sales channel.
Mumbai is the top city by sales.
74.3% of customers have at least two orders.
Return rate is 7.7%.
Cancellation rate is 9.4%.
Average delivery time for delivered orders is 4.6 days.
Aug is the highest-sales month.
Business Recommendations
1. Prioritize high-performing categories
Protect inventory availability and use targeted promotions for the strongest categories.
2. Optimize channel strategy
Maintain strong performance on the leading channel while investigating why other channels lag.
3. Improve customer retention
Use personalized offers, bundles and loyalty programs to increase repeat purchases.
4. Reduce returns
Analyze return rates by category, product, city and channel and identify common causes.
5. Improve fulfillment
Monitor delivery time by city and channel and investigate late deliveries.
6. Plan around demand patterns
Use monthly trends for inventory and campaign planning.
